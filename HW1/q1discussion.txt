The figure created demonstrates that with increasing values of alpha, 
the SEIR model approaches the SIR model. The error between these two 
curves is logarithmically related to the size of alpha.

The magnitude of E is inversely proportional to the magnitude of alpha. 
Therefore, with smaller alpha values, the SEIR curve has more latency as 
the Exposure rate has a larger impact on the behavior. As alpha gets 
larger and larger, the magnitude of the exposure curve has less and less 
impact on the infection rate.
